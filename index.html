Artin Kavoosi

I am an M.Sc. candidate in System Modeling and Data Analytics at the University of Tehran, working at the intersection of operations research and machine learning.

My interests are in decision-making under uncertainty for perishable and safety-critical supply chains — inventory control, dynamic pricing, and coordination for blood banks, fresh produce, and retail — using reinforcement learning and Markov decision processes to build methods that are theoretically grounded and usable in practice.

I am advised by Prof. Reza Tavakkoli-Moghaddam, and my thesis, “Towards autonomous supply chains for perishable products: Learning based decision making for coordination, competition, and resilience,” is expected to be completed in December 2026.

Projects

  1. Stochastic Queens Elimination (SQEP) 2026

    A stochastic, elimination-based variant of the n-queens problem, formulated as a finite-horizon MDP: a closed-form heuristic (Adaptive Coverage Greedy) with a provable worst-case approximation guarantee, and an exact MIP for the deterministic case.
